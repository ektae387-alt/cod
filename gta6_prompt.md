# GTA 6 Style — Maximum Graphics & Texture Prompt Pack
*Verified against the real GTA VI (released 19 Nov 2026, PS5 / Xbox Series X|S). Setting = Vice City + the wider state of Leonida (a fictional Florida). Protagonists = Jason Duval (ex-military, tactical, sun-tanned) & Lucia Caminos (young Latina, leather jacket, sometimes wears an ankle monitor — a Bonnie & Clyde crime duo). Aesthetic pillars = neon-soaked streets, Florida humidity/heat haze, real-time ray-traced reflections, strand-level hair with secondary motion, true-to-life sweat & makeup, wet clothing weight, dynamic weather, bayou/swamps with wildlife, and a semi-stylised-but-photoreal skin shader.*

> HOW TO USE
> - Midjourney v6.1 / Niji: paste a prompt, append `--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1` (or `--ar 21:9` for widescreen).
> - Stable Diffusion XL (Juggernaut / RealisticVision / EpicRealism) or Flux.1 Pro: use the prompt + the Negative list at the bottom.
> - For "max graphics & texture" keep the engine/quality boosters and never drop the material/lighting keywords.
